Compress PDF file size for email & WhatsApp

Shrink bulky scans so they pass upload limits.

Government and college portals in India routinely reject files over 1–5 MB. WhatsApp document caps are similarly unforgiving. Compress PDF is not optional — it is the difference between “submitted” and “upload failed”.

Online compressors (Smallpdf, iLovePDF) re-encode on their servers. Pdf Buddy compresses on your PC so a passport scan never sits in a foreign queue.

What compression actually does

  • Re-encodes embedded images (the usual bulk of a phone-scan PDF).
  • May downsample DPI — good enough for portals, not for print posters.
  • Does not magically shrink a PDF that is already only vector text.

A practical recipe

  1. Rotate and crop junk margins if your editor supports it.
  2. Compress, then open the result and check that GSTIN / signatures are still readable.
  3. If still too big, split to the pages the portal actually wants.
