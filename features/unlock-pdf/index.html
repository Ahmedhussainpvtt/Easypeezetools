Unlock PDF passwords you own & protect files

Open locked PDFs you own - and lock the ones that matter.

Unlock PDF on Pdf Buddy means: you already know the password (or you are the owner) and you want a copy you can merge, compress, or sign. It is not a cracking service.

Online “unlock PDF” sites are a magnet for other people’s files. Do not upload a password-protected board exam PDF or a locked salary register to a random compressor.

Protect vs unlock

  • Protect: set a password before you email a draft.
  • Unlock: remove that password when you need to flatten the file for a portal that hates restrictions.

Owner vs user passwords, printing restrictions, and copy restrictions are PDF permission flags - unlocking “user” restrictions still needs the right password. If you forgot it, recover from the person who set it; do not use a pirate “PDF password remover” on files you do not own.

At a glance

  • Remove passwords when you know the key
  • Add protection before sharing
  • Stay local - files don’t leave your PC for the unlock step
