Split PDF into separate files or page ranges

Pull out the pages you need - leave the rest behind.

Split PDF is the inverse of merge: extract a range, peel off a covering letter, or turn a scanned book into chapter files. Sejda-style online tools also split by bookmarks, file size, or repeating text. Pdf Buddy covers the everyday case on Windows: page ranges and selected pages, locally.

India examples

  • University wants only the marksheet page from a 12-page scan.
  • Bank wants salary slips for three months, not the whole Form 16 PDF.
  • You WhatsApp one page to a vendor without sending the full contract.

Split vs compress

Splitting reduces page count. Compressing reduces bytes on the pages you keep. Use both when a 40 MB phone scan must become a 2 MB, 2-page upload.

At a glance

  • Split by page range or extract selected pages
  • Create smaller files for email attachments
  • Works offline on your PC
