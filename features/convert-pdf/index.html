Convert to PDF — images and Office files

Turn photos and docs into shareable PDFs in one place.

The other half of “PDF tools” on iLovePDF/Smallpdf is conversion: JPG→PDF, Word→PDF, PDF→JPG. Pdf Buddy focuses on the inbound path that Indian users actually need on a PC: images and everyday Office files into a clean PDF they can then merge, compress, or sign.

JPG / PNG to PDF

Aadhaar photo, cancelled cheque, shop front photo for GST — portals want PDF, the phone gives JPEG. Convert, then compress. Put multiple images in one PDF instead of 12 attachments.

Office to PDF

When Word’s “Save as PDF” is enough, use it. When you already have mixed exports (Excel print areas, a scanned annexure, a JPG), convert and merge in Pdf Buddy so the pack is one file.

What we are not

Full PDF→editable Word with perfect layout is a hard problem (Acrobat, and some cloud converters, invest heavily here). Treat PDF→Office as “good enough to copy paragraphs,” not “pixel-identical brochure recovery.”
