What is Easy Peeze Tools?

We’re a small product company. One brand, two focused apps — documents and daily money — priced for India.

Easy Peeze Tools exists because everyday work in India still fights two annoying problems: (1) PDF chores that push you into Adobe subscriptions or upload-your-Aadhaar browser tools, and (2) UPI micro-payments that disappear from memory before they hit a ledger.

We don’t build a mega-suite. We ship small apps that do one job well, keep sensitive work local when it matters, and charge India-friendly prices instead of USD SaaS seats.

What we believe

  • Local-first for IDs & KYC — Pdf Buddy runs on Windows so Form 16s, Aadhaar packs, and bank PDFs don’t need a random cloud processor.
  • Honest expense capture — Kharch Log turns bank/UPI SMS into drafts you review, then syncs to your Google Sheet.
  • India pricing — Pdf Buddy ₹299/year or ₹999 lifetime; Kharch Log ₹149 lifetime. No seat-license theater.
  • Human support — email easypeezetools@gmail.com. Same brand for both products.

Who we’re for

Students assembling admission PDFs. Freelancers sending invoices. CAs and SMEs who hate Acrobat pricing. Households that want a simple daily kharch log without another fintech lecture.
