FAQ

What is Easy Peeze Tools?

Easy Peeze Tools is the umbrella brand behind Pdf Buddy (Windows PDF editor) and Kharch Log (Android expense tracker). Our motto: Your data stays yours - Pdf Buddy stays 100% local on your PC; Kharch Log writes to your Google Sheet.

What products do you make?

Pdf Buddy for Windows PDF work, and Kharch Log for Android expense tracking with SMS auto-capture and Google Sheets sync.

Is Pdf Buddy free to try?

Yes. Download Pdf Buddy and use it on Windows with 2 free actions per day total (edit, merge, split - shared across all tools). After that, create an account and unlock a paid plan (₹299/year or ₹999 lifetime). Files still process locally on your PC.

Where do my PDF files go?

Nowhere off your machine for core Pdf Buddy tools. It is a Windows desktop / PC app - edit, merge, split, compress, sign, OCR, unlock, convert stay 100% local. We care about your privacy.

Where does Kharch Log store expenses?

In your control: the ledger syncs to your Google Sheet in your Drive - not a locked vendor vault. Download from https://kharchlog.com.

How much do the apps cost?

Pdf Buddy: free tier (2/day after Google sign-in) then ₹299/year or ₹999 lifetime. Kharch Log: ₹149 lifetime. See /pricing/.

Can I merge, split, and compress PDFs?

Yes - merge PDF, split PDF, compress PDF, sign, OCR, unlock, convert, and edit are built into Pdf Buddy.

Adobe Acrobat alternative?

If you need everyday PDF tools without Acrobat Pro pricing - and without uploading IDs - Pdf Buddy is built for students and SMEs.

How is this different from iLovePDF or Smallpdf?

Those upload to the cloud. Pdf Buddy runs on your Windows PC so KYC and ID files never leave your disk for core tools.

One license for both apps?

Licenses are per product today. Contact us if you need both - we can help with access.

How do I contact support?

Email support@easypeeze.com.

Can Kharch Log track money I gave someone?

Yes. The Credits screen shows money given, money back, and what is still due. Learn more on kharchlog.com.

Which languages does Kharch Log support?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish, and German. Open Settings in the app and pick one. See the language list.
