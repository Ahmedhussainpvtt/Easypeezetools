What is Kharch Log?

Most of India pays by UPI now, and almost every payment sends an SMS. Kharch Log turns those SMS into a money book you never have to type.

Expense apps usually ask for one of two things: your patience, because you type every ₹20 chai and ₹60 auto ride yourself, or your bank login, so a third party can pull your statements. Kharch Log does neither. It reads the payment SMS that already land on your phone, works out the amount, the payee, the payment mode (UPI, debit card, credit card, NACH, cash) and the time, and writes a row. The parsing happens on your phone. The rows go to a Kharch Log spreadsheet in your own Google Drive, not to a server we run, so your history is yours to open, filter, chart, share with family or export to Excel any time.

On top of that ledger you get a simple home screen with your balance for the month, week or year, category insights, a search for every transaction, Credits for money you gave or borrowed, and one-tap sharing of any payment as a clean receipt. The whole app works in English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu and six more languages.

Why an SMS expense tracker works so well in India

In India nearly every digital payment triggers an SMS from your bank: UPI transfers, card swipes, auto-debits for EMIs and SIPs, salary credits and refunds. That SMS is the most complete record of your spending you already have, across every bank account and every UPI app. The problem is that it sits in your inbox, mixed with OTPs and offers, and nobody adds it up.

Kharch Log is a UPI expense tracker built on that fact. Instead of asking you to type each payment, or to link your bank through net banking or the Account Aggregator framework, it reads the alert on your phone and logs it. That is why one app can cover PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and card spends together: the bank sends the SMS no matter which app you paid with.

  • No bank login: you never share net banking passwords, card numbers or consent to pull statements.
  • On your phone: SMS are read on the device. Only the finished row (amount, payee, mode, date) goes to your Sheet.
  • Honest data: anything the app is unsure about goes to Needs review, so your totals are not padded with guesses.
  • Cash too: the + button and the voice assistant cover kirana cash, tips and anything without an SMS.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kharch Log?

Kharch Log is an Android expense tracker for India. It reads bank and UPI SMS on your phone, logs each payment within seconds, and saves every row to a Google Sheet in your own Drive. It also has manual and voice entry, a Needs review inbox, spending insights, Credits for money lent and shareable receipts.

How does Kharch Log track UPI payments automatically?

When you pay by UPI, your bank sends an SMS. Kharch Log reads that SMS on your phone, picks out the amount, payee, payment mode and time, and saves it as an expense. It works the same for PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and BHIM because the bank sends the alert.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. Kharch Log never asks for net banking passwords, card details or Account Aggregator consent. It only uses the SMS your bank already sends you.

How fast does a payment show up?

Usually within a few seconds of the bank SMS arriving, and you get a notification you can tap to open that transaction. If your phone's battery saver blocks background work, allow Kharch Log to run in the background.

Where is my data stored?

In a Kharch Log spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. The app can only access the files it creates. We do not keep a copy of your expenses on our servers.

What happens with SMS it cannot read clearly?

They go to the Needs review inbox. You approve, edit or reject each one, so nothing is guessed. OTPs and promotional SMS are ignored.

Can I add cash expenses?

Yes. Tap + to add any expense or income with category, payment mode, payee and notes, or use the voice assistant and just say it.

Can I track money I lent to friends?

Yes. Credits keeps money given, money back and what is still due for each person, with a due date reminder, a one-tap WhatsApp reminder and a PDF bill with your UPI QR.

Can I share a payment receipt?

Yes. Open any transaction and tap Share to send a clean receipt with the amount, date, mode and UPI reference, or save it as a PDF bill with your UPI ID and a Pay QR.

How much does Kharch Log cost?

₹149 one time for lifetime access. There is no subscription, no premium tier and no ads.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Urdu, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish and German. Pick one in Settings and the whole app follows.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. iPhone does not allow apps to read SMS, so Kharch Log is Android only. Your Google Sheet still opens on iPhone and the web.

Can I export my expenses to Excel?

Yes. Your data is already a Google Sheet, so you can download it as Excel or CSV, or share it with family or your CA, any time.
