What is Easy Peeze Tools?

Your data stays yours. One brand, two focused apps — documents and daily money — built so you keep control.

Easy Peeze Tools exists because everyday work still fights two annoying problems: (1) PDF chores that push you into Adobe subscriptions or upload-your-docs browser tools, and (2) UPI micro-payments that disappear from memory before they hit a ledger you own.

We don’t build a mega-suite. We ship small apps that do one job well, put privacy first, and charge fair prices instead of USD SaaS seats.

Our privacy motto

  • Pdf Buddy — 100% local on Windows / PC. Core PDF work (edit, merge, split, compress, sign, OCR, unlock, convert) runs on your desktop. Files stay on your machine. We care about your privacy — especially for KYC, Form 16, and bank PDFs.
  • Kharch Log — your data stays yours. Expenses sync to your Google Sheet in your Drive. Not a black-box cloud ledger you can’t export.
  • Try Pdf Buddy free: download and use 3 actions per day total (edit, merge, split — shared). Need more? Create an account and unlock yearly or lifetime.
  • Fair pricing — after the free daily limit: Pdf Buddy ₹299/year or ₹999 lifetime; Kharch Log ₹149 lifetime.
  • Human support — email easypeezetools@gmail.com.

Who we’re for

Students assembling admission PDFs. Freelancers sending invoices. CAs and SMEs who hate Acrobat pricing — and hate uploading IDs. Households that want a simple daily kharch log in their own Sheet.
