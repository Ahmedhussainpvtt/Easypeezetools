FAQ — Easy Peeze Tools

Pdf Buddy, Kharch Log, pricing, privacy, and Adobe alternatives.

What is Easy Peeze Tools?

Easy Peeze Tools is the brand behind Pdf Buddy (Windows PDF editor) and Kharch Log (Android expense tracker). Motto: Your data stays yours.

What does “your data stays yours” mean?

Pdf Buddy processes PDFs 100% locally on your Windows PC. Kharch Log syncs expenses to your Google Sheet in your Drive — not a locked vendor vault.

Is Pdf Buddy free?

Yes to start: download and use 3 free actions per day total (shared across all tools). After that, create an account and unlock yearly (₹299) or lifetime (₹999).

Where do my PDF files go?

They stay on your PC for core Pdf Buddy tools. We care about your privacy — especially for KYC and bank documents.

How much does Kharch Log cost?

Kharch Log lifetime access is ₹149 — see /pricing/#kharch-log. Your ledger lives in your Google Sheet.

What products do you sell?

Pdf Buddy on this site, and Kharch Log at https://kharchlog.in. Pricing for both is on /pricing/.

Is Pdf Buddy an online PDF editor?

No — core tools run as a Windows desktop app so files stay on your PC. The website is for download, docs, and guides.

What PDF tools are included?

Edit, merge, split, compress, sign, OCR, unlock/protect, and convert.

Does it replace Adobe Acrobat?

For everyday edit/merge/sign/compress/OCR — yes for most students and SMEs. Acrobat still wins for niche pro publishing features.

Which OS is supported for Pdf Buddy?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

How is this different from iLovePDF or Smallpdf?

They are browser tools: upload, process, download. Pdf Buddy runs on your Windows PC so KYC and ID files do not have to hit a public processor.

Do I need one account for both apps?

Licenses are per product. Email us if you use both and need help activating access.

How do I get support?

Email easypeezetools@gmail.com.
