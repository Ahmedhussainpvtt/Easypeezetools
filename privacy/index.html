Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05 - Easy Peeze Tools - Contact: info@easypeeze.com

Important: This policy is written for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and related rules, plus Play/desktop store expectations. It is not legal advice. If you need formal counsel for your situation, consult a lawyer.

Your data stays yours. This Privacy Policy is the canonical notice for Easy Peeze Tools and all products we operate:

  • easypeeze.com - company website, guides, blog, pricing, checkout
  • Pdf Buddy - Windows desktop PDF editor (local-first)
  • Kharch Log - Android expense tracker (kharchlog.com and related apps)
  • License / payment backends that unlock paid plans

1. Who we are (Data Fiduciary)

For personal data we control in connection with licenses, websites, and support, Easy Peeze Tools acts as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act.

  • Brand / operator: Easy Peeze Tools
  • Grievance / privacy email: info@easypeeze.com
  • Sites: https://easypeeze.com - https://kharchlog.com

Google (Sign-In, Drive/Sheets), Razorpay/Cashfree (payments), and hosting providers process data as independent controllers or processors under their own terms where applicable.

2. Privacy principles

  • Pdf Buddy - core PDF work (edit, merge, split, compress, sign, OCR, unlock, convert) runs locally on your Windows PC. We do not upload your PDF file contents to Easy Peeze servers for those tools.
  • Kharch Log - when Sheets sync is on, expense rows live in your Google Sheet / Drive. We do not sell your ledger or use it for ads.
  • We do not sell personal data to third-party advertisers.

2a. How we use Google account access (Kharch Log)

Kharch Log uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive / Sheets so your expense ledger lives in your Google account - not a proprietary cloud you can't export.

  • Sign-In - identify your Google account so we can attach a paid license and open your Sheet.
  • Drive / Sheets - create or update the expense spreadsheet and folder Kharch Log makes for you. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.
  • What we don't do - we don't sell your ledger, don't use Sheet contents for ads, and don't need Google access for Pdf Buddy's local PDF tools.
  • Revoke anytime - Google Account -> Third-party access, or uninstall Kharch Log.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Kharch Log receives your Google email address, display name, and profile picture information.

We use this information to create and manage your Kharch Log account, display your profile, and personalize account-related communications. Your profile picture is displayed in your Kharch Log profile.

Kharch Log does not store a copy of your profile picture. The profile picture information associated with your account is deleted when you delete your Kharch Log account, subject to any limited retention described in this Privacy Policy.

Product site: kharchlog.com - Support: support@easypeeze.com

3. Personal data we process (itemised)

3.1 Account, licensing & checkout

  • Google account email, display name, and profile picture information
  • Checkout fields you enter: first name, optional last name, email, phone (with country code)
  • License / plan status (free, yearly, lifetime) per product
  • Payment details from the payment provider: reference, amount, currency, status, and time. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Support emails and attachments you send us
  • Your choice to stop marketing emails, when you use the unsubscribe option or email support@easypeeze.com

3.2 Technical / security signals

  • App version, operating system (Windows or Android), approximate IP address, time, and signals used to detect abuse
  • The Play Store version of Kharch Log may send crash and diagnostic information, such as device information, app version, and crash details, so we can fix problems. Those reports do not include your email, SMS text, or expense amounts. The website download does not send crash reports.
  • Kharch Log app usage via Google Analytics (which screens you open, app version, device and operating system). This does not include SMS text or expense amounts, and advertising ID collection is off.
  • Website hosting logs (page address, browser, referrer, IP) kept by our website host
  • Website usage via Google Analytics on easypeeze.com and kharchlog.com: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. Not form passwords or PDF contents.

3.3 Pdf Buddy (on-device)

  • PDF contents and paths stay on your device for core editing
  • Local preferences (UI, free-action counters)
  • After sign-in, Pdf Buddy checks whether your account is paid. That check uses your account email.
  • Analytics: the desktop app uses the same Google Analytics as easypeeze.com. It records app opens, mode changes, saves, sign-in and sign-out, and upgrade clicks, plus app version. It does not receive PDF contents, file names, or your email.

3.4 Kharch Log (Android)

  • Expense fields you enter (amount, category, mode, notes, person, etc.)
  • SMS (optional): if you grant SMS permission, the app reads bank/UPI-related messages on-device to draft expenses. Drafts go to your review inbox and/or your Google Sheet. We do not operate a raw-SMS upload service for ads or resale.
  • Google Drive/Sheets: the expense files Kharch Log creates, or a spreadsheet you pick for the app. Kharch Log does not get access to your whole Drive.
  • Microphone (optional): only for voice entry; speech uses on-device / OS services
  • Local app preferences and notification settings
  • Analytics: Google Analytics in the app records opens and screens so we can fix bugs and see what features are used. It does not receive your SMS text or expense amounts. Advertising ID collection is off.
  • Account and marketing emails: creating an account enrolls you to receive account and marketing emails from Easy Peeze. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option that allows you to stop receiving marketing communications. You can also contact us at support@easypeeze.com to opt out of marketing emails.

3.5 Data we do not intentionally collect

  • Full card numbers or card security codes on our servers
  • Advertising IDs for cross-app tracking networks
  • Contacts / photo libraries except where an OS picker is used for a feature you start (e.g. image to PDF on device)

4. Purposes of processing

We process personal data only for these purposes:

  • Service delivery - sign-in, licenses, free-tier limits, paid unlocks, sync features you enable
  • Payments - create orders/subscriptions, confirm payment, attach license to the email you provide
  • Support - answer tickets and fix bugs
  • Security & integrity - prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Legal compliance - respond to lawful requests and enforce our Terms
  • Product reliability - Play Store crash and diagnostic reports, plus Google Analytics on the websites, in Pdf Buddy, and in Kharch Log, to see how the products are used. These are not used for ads.

We do not use your expense ledger or PDF contents for targeted advertising.

5. Consent, notice & withdrawal (DPDP)

  • By creating an account, granting permissions (SMS, mic, Drive), or completing checkout, you give informed consent for the related purposes above. Creating an account also enrolls you to receive account and marketing emails from Easy Peeze. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option, or you can email support@easypeeze.com to opt out of marketing emails.
  • You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it, for example: use the unsubscribe option in a marketing email, or email support@easypeeze.com; delete the Kharch Log account in the app; revoke Google access at Google Account -> Third-party access; disable SMS/mic in Android settings; uninstall the app.
  • Withdrawing consent may stop related features (e.g. SMS capture or Sheets sync) and may affect paid unlock verification if we can no longer associate your license.
  • Some processing is necessary to provide the service you request (e.g. storing that your email is licensed after you pay).

6. Your rights (Data Principal)

Subject to the DPDP Act and applicable exceptions, you may request:

  • Access - confirmation of what account/license data we hold about you
  • Correction - fix inaccurate account or checkout details we control
  • Erasure - delete license/account records we store, where we are not required to retain them for fraud prevention, taxes, or law
  • Grievance redressal - complain to us via the contact below
  • Nomination - nominate another individual to exercise rights in case of death or incapacity (email us to register a nominee)

How to exercise rights: email info@easypeeze.com with subject line “DPDP request” and enough detail to verify you control the account email. We will respond within a reasonable period.

You may also complain to India’s Data Protection Board of India as and when that mechanism is available under the Act/Rules.

Kharch Log deletion: when you delete your account in the app, we delete your name, email, expenses, plan, and the phones signed in to that account, and we remove Kharch Log's access to your Google Sheet. We do not keep your email on that deletion. We keep limited security records, without your email or expenses, for 1 year so we can look into abuse, then we delete them. You can sign up again with the same Google account. That is a new account.

Sheet data: expense rows in your Google Sheet are under your Google account - edit/delete them there or revoke app access. Account deletion also removes the Kharch Log sheet and folder from Drive when the app can still reach them.

7. Sharing & processors

  • Google - sign-in, and Drive and Sheets access for Kharch Log features you turn on. Google Analytics and, on the Play Store version, crash and diagnostic reports.
  • Razorpay / Cashfree (or similar) - payment processing
  • Amplitude - how visits move through easypeeze.com and kharchlog.com. It does not receive your expenses or PDF contents.
  • Hosting - Google Cloud hosts the account and payment service. The websites are static pages on our website host.
  • Legal / safety - if required by law or to protect rights, users, or the public

If you share your Google Sheet with others, those people can see the expense data - that is under your control.

8. Cross-border processing

We primarily operate from India. Infrastructure or vendors may process data in other countries. By using the products you understand personal data may be transferred/stored in such locations subject to applicable law and vendor safeguards.

9. Retention

  • License / payment metadata - retained while the account exists, and for a reasonable period afterward for fraud prevention, accounting, and legal claims
  • After a Kharch Log account deletion - your name, email, expenses, plan, and signed-in phones are deleted at that time. Limited security records, without your email or expenses, are kept for 1 year so we can look into abuse, then deleted.
  • Marketing emails - they stop when you use the unsubscribe option in a marketing email, or when you email support@easypeeze.com to opt out
  • Support emails - retained as needed to resolve issues
  • Website logs - short-lived operational retention by the host
  • On-device data - until you clear app data / uninstall; PDFs and Sheets remain where you saved them

10. Security

We use HTTPS and limit access to what each feature needs. No method is completely secure. Protect your Google account with a strong password and 2FA.

11. Children

Products are not directed at children under 18. Do not use them if you are under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us for deletion.

12. Cookies & similar technologies

easypeeze.com and kharchlog.com use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited, plus device and approximate location. The same sites use Amplitude to see how a visit moves through the pages. Amplitude does not receive your expenses or PDF contents. We do not use an advertising ID in the Kharch Log app, and we do not run an ad network. Checkout pages may keep your email in the browser only long enough to finish payment.

13. Automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you beyond routine license entitlement checks (paid vs free).

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes may also be called out on the product sites. Continued use after the update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact / grievance

Privacy & grievance: info@easypeeze.com
