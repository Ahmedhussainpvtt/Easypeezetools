Form 16 PDF — split pages, compress, merge for ITR packs

How to split Form 16 PDF pages, compress under portal limits, and merge with other KYC docs — desktop, no upload.

Form 16 arrives as a multi-page PDF. Portals often want Part A / Part B separately, or a compressed pack with salary slips.

Typical workflow

  1. Split Form 16 into the pages the portal asks for.
  2. Compress if the upload cap is 1–2 MB.
  3. Merge with bank statements / photo only if the checklist says so.

Pdf Buddy does this offline — do not upload Form 16 to random “free PDF” sites. Glossary: Form 16 PDF.
