Compress PDF for WhatsApp and 2 MB Indian portals

WhatsApp PDF size limits and Indian portal 2 MB caps — how to compress PDF without wrecking PAN/signature readability.

Phone scans easily hit 15–25 MB. WhatsApp and many Indian portals (jobs, banks, colleges) demand ~2 MB or less.

Fast recipe

  1. Split to only the pages you need.
  2. Run Compress PDF in Pdf Buddy (local).
  3. Open the result — zoom PAN, photo, signature.
  4. If still huge, lower scan DPI next time (200–300 DPI is enough for forms).
