Rotate PDF pages on Windows before you merge

2026-09-04 · rotate PDF, merge PDF, Windows

Half the “upload failed” tickets on Indian portals are actually human rejection: a sideways Aadhaar photo inside a PDF. Before you merge or compress, rotate pages so text reads upright.

Quick checklist

  1. Open the scan in Pdf Buddy.
  2. Rotate each sideways page 90° until the header is on top.
  3. Save, then merge into your KYC pack.

If the portal still rejects, check MB limits next — not orientation.
