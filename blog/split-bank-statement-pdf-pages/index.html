Split bank statement PDF pages before you upload

2026-09-12 · split PDF, bank statement, privacy

Job and loan forms often ask for “last 3 months” - not your entire PDF dump. Splitting first is faster than compressing a monster file.

Clean split checklist

  1. Open the statement PDF on Windows.
  2. Use Split for the exact page range.
  3. Optional: OCR if you need Ctrl+F later.
  4. Only then compress for the portal.

Privacy note

Full statements show balances, UPI counterparties, and salary credits. Prefer local split/edit - same reason we advise against upload-based editors.
