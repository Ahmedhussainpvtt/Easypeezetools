Best PDF editor for Windows in India (2026)

2026-09-01 · PDF editor, Windows, India

Search “best PDF editor for Windows” and you get Acrobat Pro, a carousel of free online PDF editors (iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda, pdfFiller), and abandoned freeware. If you actually work in India - GST, KYC, university portals, CA working papers - the buying question is narrower:

  • Can I merge, split, compress, sign, and OCR without a US-dollar subscription?
  • Does a passport PDF have to hit someone else’s S3 bucket first?
  • Will the tool still work when the hostel Wi-Fi dies?

Pdf Buddy by Easy Peeze Tools is a Windows desktop editor at ₹299/year or ₹999 lifetime. It is not trying to be Salesforce-grade document ops. It is trying to replace the five browser tabs you open every admission season.

What Indians actually need from a PDF editor

  • Merge PDF for multi-page applications (IIM, PSU, bank KYC).
  • Compress PDF because “maximum 2 MB” is a personality trait of Indian portals.
  • Sign PDF for offers and vendor forms when a DSC is overkill.
  • OCR PDF for phone scans of paper invoices and marksheets.
  • Files that stay on the PC when the document is an ID or a client file.

Online PDF editors vs desktop

Cloud editors are excellent UX: drop a file, get a result, no install. The tradeoffs are documented in their own UIs - hourly task limits, 50 MB caps, “files deleted after 2 hours,” and explicit notes that scanned text cannot be edited as text. Sejda even sells a desktop app for people who refuse to upload.

Pdf Buddy is that desktop idea with India pricing and a single brand (Easy Peeze Tools) instead of a Swiss/US SaaS invoice.

Acrobat vs Pdf Buddy

Acrobat Pro still wins at prepress, some accessibility tagging, and enterprise admin. Most students and SMEs never touch those. They need “fix this PDF and get it under 2 MB.” That is Pdf Buddy.
