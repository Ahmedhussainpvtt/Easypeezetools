Make a scanned PDF searchable with OCR

2026-09-03 · OCR PDF, searchable PDF

A phone scan is often just a picture inside a PDF - Ctrl+F finds nothing. OCR PDF adds a text layer so the file becomes a searchable PDF. That matters for CA working papers, GST invoice archives, and any folder you will grep six months later.

Deep dive: OCR with Pdf Buddy.

OCR quality checklist

  • Light even, no motion blur, page filling the frame.
  • Language pack matches the document (English vs mixed Indic scripts - results vary).
  • Always spot-check PAN, GSTIN, and amounts. OCR is a search aid, not a statutory copy.
