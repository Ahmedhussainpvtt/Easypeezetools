Pdf Buddy vs iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda & pdfFiller

2026-09-03 · PDF editor, iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda, pdfFiller

Four names dominate “edit PDF online” search: iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Sejda, and pdfFiller. They are good products. Pdf Buddy is not a clone of them. Here is when each shape of tool wins.

What the online suites optimize for

  • iLovePDF / Smallpdf: a grid of single-purpose tools (merge, compress, convert). Fast, familiar, install-free.
  • Sejda: a serious in-browser editor (type on PDF, forms, sign) plus an optional desktop app if you refuse uploads. Free tiers have task/hour and size caps.
  • pdfFiller: cloud document ops — fillable forms, collaboration, eSign workflows, industry templates. Built for teams, not a one-off compress.

What Pdf Buddy optimizes for

  • Windows 10/11, files stay on the PC for core edit/merge/split/compress/sign/OCR.
  • India pricing: ₹149/year or ₹999 lifetime instead of stacked USD seats.
  • Portal reality: GST, college, bank KYC packs that must be one PDF under a hard MB cap.
