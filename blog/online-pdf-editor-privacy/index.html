Why upload-based online PDF editors are risky

2026-09-03 · privacy, online PDF editor

Free online PDF editors ask you to upload first. Their privacy pages usually say files are encrypted in transit, processed, then deleted after a few hours. That is better than a random Telegram bot. It is still a copy of your Aadhaar on a server you do not control, in a jurisdiction you did not pick, under a ToS you did not read.
