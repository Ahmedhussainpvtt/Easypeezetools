PDF size limits for EPFO, bank & gov portals (practical compress)

2026-09-14 - compress PDF, EPFO, portal limits, India

Competitor guides cite the same ranges: many bank KYC uploads ~1-2 MB; some gov flows (including stricter EPFO-style caps) go as low as ~500 KB. Always read the live portal text - limits change.

Compress without wrecking verification

  1. Remove extra pages first (split).
  2. Merge only what is required.
  3. Compress on Windows; compare side-by-side at 100% zoom.
  4. If still huge, re-scan grayscale at lower DPI - then convert again.
